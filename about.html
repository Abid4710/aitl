About AITL

A Bangladesh-based IT solutions and technology engineering company. working across software, enterprise, infrastructure, telecom and healthcare.

Company overview

Advanced Intelligent Technology (AITL) is a full-service IT solutions and technology engineering company. We provide custom software, enterprise systems, web development, AI, government technology, road infrastructure, healthcare and telecom solutions, and we are not limited to one programming language, framework or industry. Our goal is to understand client requirements, design suitable technical solutions, develop software, integrate systems and support digital transformation.
